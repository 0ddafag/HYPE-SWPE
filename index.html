HYPE / Supply-Weighted P-E

SWPE dashboard for Hyperliquid

SWPE compares HYPE's supply-adjusted market cap to protocol earnings that are used for buybacks. Lower values suggest stronger buyback demand relative to float and can support the case for undervaluation.

SWPE = selected supply x price / (30d EMA buyback revenue x 365)

Method

How the calculation works

  • Ready-for-Sale supply = circulating supply minus staked HYPE.
  • Current circulating staked HYPE = total staked minus the unstaking queue minus live delegated stake at the HyperLabs and Foundation excluded wallets.
  • Buyback revenue uses DefiLlama daily holders revenue for the live series, then falls back to CSV buybacks, buyback_fee_allocation, or revenue.
  • SWPE uses 30d EMA daily revenue annualized by multiplying by 365.
  • Mean SWPE is the selected timeframe average of SWPE shown as a flat reference line.
  • AQAv2-adjusted scenario uses a live provisional Coinbase-address USDC balance proxy (HyperEVM ERC-20 plus HyperCore spot USDC), then applies daily balance x reserve yield x approximately 90% protocol share after the 2026-08-26 activation date. The proxy is not the final eligible-balance formula. Each completed 30-day cash batch is modeled as batch / 30 per day beginning on the reported 2026-10-03 first-payment date, with price and supply held flat beyond the observed series.

Source Notes

Practical free / cheap stack

  • CoinGecko Demo API for live price and supply refreshes on each page load or button click.
  • DefiLlama for free Hyperliquid revenue and holders-revenue series.
  • Hyperliquid official API for validator summaries and the network-wide total staked number.
  • Hypurrscan for the public unstaking queue and staking cross-checks.
  • AQAv2 balance proxy reads USDC ERC-20 balances on HyperEVM and USDC spot balances on HyperCore for the two Coinbase activation addresses. This is a live provisional proxy pending the exact on-chain AQAv2 eligible-balance formula.
